- **Focus:** Pedagogical engineering curriculum, structured progression, 4 pillars (Foundation $\rightarrow$ Firmware $\rightarrow$ Systems $\rightarrow$ Career), 8 sequential steps, 6-month weekly study plan, 7 portfolio projects, interview checklists, and common engineering mistakes.
