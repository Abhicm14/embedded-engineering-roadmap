# 🎓 Curated Online Courses & Video Lectures

> Structured university courses and professional hands-on video series (👶 Beginner-friendly, 💎 In-depth reference).

---

## 1. University & Comprehensive Courses

- 🎞️ 👶 [**Embedded Systems - Shape the World (UTAustinX / edX)**](https://www.edx.org/course/embedded-systems-shape-the-world-microcontroller-i) by Prof. Jonathan Valvano & Ramesh Yerraballi (UT Austin)  
  *Focus:* ARM Cortex-M architecture, GPIO, SysTick, timers, ADC, DAC, UART, sound generation, and state machines using physical TM4C123 LaunchPad boards.
- 🎞️ 👶 [**Modern Embedded Systems Programming**](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLPW8O6W-1chwyTzI3BHwBLbGQoPFxPAPM) by Dr. Miro Samek (Quantum Leaps)  
  *Focus:* Outstanding free 30-lecture YouTube series teaching bare-metal ARM Cortex-M from the ground up: pointers to registers, interrupt handling, stack frame anatomy, RTOS concepts, and oscilloscope verification.
- 🎞️ 💎 [**Introduction to Embedded Systems Software and Development Environments**](https://www.coursera.org/learn/embedded-software-hardware) by University of Colorado Boulder (Coursera)  
  *Focus:* Build systems, GCC cross-compilation toolchains, linker scripts, memory architecture, and writing hardware test harnesses.

## 2. Professional & Specialized Video Tracks

- 🎞️ 👶 [**FastBit Embedded Brain Academy (Udemy)**](https://www.udemy.com/user/kiran-nayak-2/) by Kiran Nayak  
  *Courses:*
  - *Embedded C: Absolute Beginners to Advanced* 👶
  - *ARM Cortex-M3/M4 Processor Architecture* 💎
  - *Mastering Microcontroller with STM32 Peripherals* 👶
  - *Mastering RTOS: Hands-on FreeRTOS and STM32* 💎
  *Focus:* Deep, step-by-step register level walkthroughs using STM32 Nucleo boards, Keil/STM32CubeIDE, and logic analyzers.
- 🎞️ 💎 [**Linux Foundation: Developing Linux Device Drivers (LFD430)**](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/training/developing-linux-device-drivers/)  
  *Focus:* Official Linux Foundation curriculum covering character device drivers, kernel synchronization, platform buses, Device Trees, and memory management.
- 🎞️ 👶 [**CS50: Introduction to Computer Science (Harvard / edX)**](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) by David J. Malan  
  *Focus:* The premier foundational introduction to computational thinking, C programming, memory pointers, and binary data representation.
